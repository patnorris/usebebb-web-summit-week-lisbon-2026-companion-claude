## 1. Why networking at Web Summit fails

- 200+ side events are announced; only a fraction matter for any one goal.
- The best founder and investor rooms need approval, an application or an invitation.
- Evening events on 9–11 Nov overlap.
- Random conversations mean you miss the one investor who mattered.
- Follow-ups die after the closing keynote on Thu 12 Nov.

## 2. Side events: the basics

- **Not included with the Web Summit ticket.** The ticket covers Web Summit and each night of Night Summit (Mon–Wed). Source: [Web Summit Schedule Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule-essentials/)
- Side events are independent and run their own registration, usually on Luma, Eventbrite or their own site. Portugal Tech Week events (for example at [PTW Palace](https://portugaltechweek.com/portugal-tech-week-palace/)) also register separately.
- **An RSVP is not an approval.** Many events show "approval required": registering puts you in a queue, the organizer decides.
- Pick by **objective first, then access**.

## 4. Events by objective

- **Fundraise**: Venture Night (Tue 10 Nov), Bridging Capital Across Borders (Wed 11 Nov afternoon), Foundacious Lisbon (Wed 11 Nov evening, German angels). Start with approval-based investor rooms and apply early.
- **Build / meet AI builders**: Build AI Agents with Dust (Mon 9 Nov).
- **Find peers**: Founders Night #8 (Mon 9 Nov), Superconnectors (Tue 10 Nov).
- **First-time orientation**: First Timers meetup (Tue 10 Nov, 11:00).
- **Breadth / general mingling**: Night Summit (Mon–Wed), included with the ticket.

Possible evening pairings (primary · backup), to adapt to the user's goal and access:
- Mon 9 Nov: Founders Night #8 (18:00, paid) · Build AI Agents with Dust (19:30, approval), or Night Summit.
- Tue 10 Nov: Venture Night (18:00) or Superconnectors (17:00), each the other's backup depending on goal; Night Summit as fallback if approvals don't come through.
- Wed 11 Nov: Foundacious Lisbon (19:00, approval) · Night Summit. Bridging Capital Across Borders is an afternoon slot (15:30), which can work alongside the Web Summit day.

## 5. Overlap hotspots (snapshot 20 Oct)

- **Mon 9 Nov, 18:00–21:30**: the biggest hotspot, about five founder and AI events clash. Example: Founders Night #8 (18:00, paid) vs Build AI Agents with Dust (19:30, free, approval).
- **Tue 10 Nov, 17:00–23:00**: e.g. Superconnectors (17:00) and Venture Night (18:00–24:00) overlap.
- **Wed 11 Nov evening**.

When the user names an event, check it against these and against other events in their plan, flag the clash and propose a backup.

## 7. Night Summit or a side event?

- Choose **one plan per evening**: Night Summit for breadth, a curated side event for a precise goal, or rest.
- Night Summit runs Mon–Wed evenings at different locations, included with the ticket; wear the lanyard and wristband. Schedule and locations appear in the official app. Sources: [Web Summit Schedule Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule-essentials/), [Night Summit page](https://websummit.com/night-summit/)
- Side events offer smaller, goal-specific rooms but need RSVPs.
- A strong evening: a side event, then a cocktail or rooftop bar for deeper conversations.

## 8. Investor events

- Most investor events during the week need an application or approval. **Apply 1–2 weeks ahead and keep a backup.**
- Some rooms are investor-only or invitation-only.
- Examples: Superconnectors (Tue 10 Nov, 17:00, free, approval), Bridging Capital Across Borders (Wed 11 Nov, 15:30, PTW Palace, approval), Venture Night (Tue 10 Nov, approval for startup tickets), Foundacious Lisbon (Wed 11 Nov, 19:00, approval).
- For more investor events: [VNTR](https://os.vntr.vc/side-events/web-summit-2026).

## 9. Specific people

- Nobody (not Bebb, not this skill) can promise that a specific CEO, investor or speaker will attend or meet the user.
- For a targeted ask, suggest organizer-run formats such as Superconnectors (founders pitch one critical ask to the room; approval required), and goal-based opt-in matching.
- Speaker lists: [official speakers page](https://websummit.com/speakers/). Being on it doesn't mean someone takes meetings.

## 10. Follow-up

- Follow up **within 48 hours, with context** (what you talked about, the specific next step). Context fades fast after Thu 12 Nov.
- Keep every lead in one place (the "1 pipeline"): names, what was promised, pending RSVPs.

## 11. The 3-2-1 method

- **3**: launch a networking agent (or brief your assistant) in under 3 minutes, before you land: what you're building, whom you need, when you're free. Brief on goals, not job titles.
- **2**: use 2 sources of connections: opt-in people matches plus curated side events.
- **1**: keep 1 pipeline that keeps working after Lisbon.

## 1. Why networking at Web Summit fails

- 200+ side events are announced; only a fraction matter for any one goal.
- The best founder and investor rooms need approval, an application or an invitation.
- Evening events on 9–11 Nov overlap.
- Random conversations mean you miss the one investor who mattered.
- Follow-ups die after the closing keynote on Thu 12 Nov.

## 2. Side events: the basics

- **Not included with the Web Summit ticket.** The ticket covers Web Summit and each night of Night Summit (Mon–Wed). Source: [Web Summit Schedule Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule-essentials/)
- Side events are independent and run their own registration, usually on Luma, Eventbrite or their own site. Portugal Tech Week events (for example at [PTW Palace](https://portugaltechweek.com/portugal-tech-week-palace/), Rua Gomes Freire 98, Arroios; programme: https://portugaltechweek.com/palace-events/) also register separately.
- **An RSVP is not an approval.** "Approval required" means registering puts you in a queue; the organizer decides. "Full, waitlist" means no guaranteed place.
- Pick by **objective first, then access**.

## 5. Overlap hotspots (checked 5 Nov)

- **Mon 9 Nov, 18:00–21:30**: the biggest hotspot; several founder and AI events clash. Example: Founders Night #8 (18:00, paid) vs Build AI Agents with Dust (19:30, free, waitlist).
- **Tue 10 Nov, 17:30–24:00**: e.g. Superconnectors (17:30–19:30), Venture Night (18:00–24:00), Physical AI: Demo or Die (18:00–21:00), Can Europe Lead in AI? (19:30).
- **Wed 11 Nov, from mid-afternoon into the night**: e.g. Bridging Capital (15:30), From Automation to Autonomy (17:30), AI Horizon Conference (18:00), Foundacious (19:00).

When the user names an event, check it against these and the other events in their plan, flag the clash and propose a backup.

## 7. Night Summit or a side event?

- Choose **one plan per evening**: Night Summit for breadth, a curated side event for a precise goal, or rest.
- Night Summit runs Mon–Wed evenings at different locations, included with the ticket; wear the lanyard and wristband. Schedule and locations are in the official app. Sources: [Web Summit Schedule Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule-essentials/), [Night Summit page](https://websummit.com/night-summit/)
- Mon 9 Nov also has **Opening Night**, 18:00–20:00, Centre Stage (the conference kick-off).
- Side events offer smaller, goal-specific rooms but need RSVPs.
- A strong evening: a side event, then a cocktail or rooftop bar for deeper conversations.

## 8. Investor events

- Most investor events during the week need an application or approval. **Apply 1–2 weeks ahead and keep a backup**; during the week, apply immediately and plan the backup.
- Some rooms are investor-only or invitation-only.
- Examples (checked 5 Nov): Superconnectors (Tue 10 Nov, 17:30, free, approval), Bridging Capital Across Borders (Wed 11 Nov, 15:30, PTW Palace, approval), Venture Night (Tue 10 Nov, approval to pitch), Foundacious Lisbon (Wed 11 Nov, 19:00, approval). Side-event talk: US Fundraising Playbook for European Founders (Thu 12 Nov, 16:00, PTW Palace).
- On the conference side: Rethinking venture capital (Antler / Cleo Capital), Tue 10 Nov, 10:35–11:00, Venture Stage 5.
- For more investor events: [VNTR](https://os.vntr.vc/side-events/web-summit-2026).

## 10. Specific people

- Nobody (not Bebb, not this skill) can promise that a specific CEO, investor or speaker will attend or meet the user.
- For a targeted ask, suggest organizer-run formats such as Superconnectors (founders pitch one critical ask; approval required), and goal-based opt-in matching.
- Speaker lists: [official speakers page](https://websummit.com/speakers/). Being on it doesn't mean someone takes meetings.

## 11. Follow-up

- Follow up **within 48 hours, with context**. Context fades fast after Thu 12 Nov.
- Keep every lead in one place (the "1 pipeline"). The full method is in `relationship-management.md`.

## 12. The 3-2-1 method

- **3**: launch a networking agent (or brief your assistant) in under 3 minutes: what you're building, whom you need, when you're free. Brief on goals, not job titles.
- **2**: use 2 sources of connections: opt-in people matches plus curated side events.
- **1**: keep 1 pipeline that keeps working after Lisbon.

## 4. Hands-on demos with booth numbers

From the exhibitors', university and research teams' own announcements. Booths can move; check the official app on site. A demo makes an easy opener: try it, then ask one sharp question.

- [Xynova Prima 1 / Flex 2](https://xynova.ai/web-summit-2026) (robotic hands): hands-on manipulation; visitors can teleoperate the hands. Booth E109, 10–12 Nov.
- [Técnico ULisboa](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/pt/noticias/campus-e-comunidade/tecnico-leva-demonstracoes-tecnologicas-e-spin-offs-a-web-summit-2026/) (robots and VR): Lola and Unitree robots, plus the Echo of the Abyss deep-sea VR experience. Pavilion 2, E272, 10–12 Nov.
- [Mudita Kompakt](https://mudita.com/about/media/mudita-to-showcase-mudita-kompakt-and-inkapps-at-web-summit-2026/) (E-ink phone): a minimalist smartphone and InkApps. Stand E106.
- [norriv](https://www.norriv.com/blogs/norriv-holographic-ai-automation-web-summit-2026) (holographic AI): Hologram Boxes with conversational avatars and payment integration. Pavilion 1, E107.
- [PERSEPHONE](https://www.persephone-mining.eu/news/e5m435n7m7d72vujlfokqgqwyj852e) (industrial autonomy): research demos for robotic underground mining. Wed 11 Nov, 09:00–12:00, EU-funded projects area, Pavilion 4.
- EU research showcase by HaDEA (RISC-V and materials): RISER's energy-efficient RISC-V cloud work and AI-TranspWood's AI-assisted transparent wood. Pavilion 4, E418.
- [Intel](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/artificial-intelligence/intel-at-web-summit-2026.html) (physical and edge AI): sensing, on-device inference and real-time control for robotics and industry. Pavilion 2, E267, 10–12 Nov.
- [Akamai Cloud](https://web.cvent.com/event/4dc96a7c-35ee-4ac5-8e53-7d6e93990530/summary) (AI infrastructure): distributed compute and cloud orchestration for latency-sensitive AI. Pavilion 4, E467.

## 5. Startups worth a visit

Picked from the [2026 featured startups](https://websummit.com/startups/featured-startups/search/). Featured status doesn't confirm a booth day or a public demo; find on-site details in the official app.

- **Ganiga.ai / Hoooly** (physical AI, circular economy): vision-guided bins that sort waste and measure disposal.
- **Involve Space** (space, sensing): autonomous stratospheric platforms for Earth observation, between drones and satellites.
- **OroraTech** (climate, space): thermal satellite imagery for wildfire monitoring. KPMG finalist, Wed 11 Nov.
- **GetVocal AI** (enterprise agents): voice and chat agents built into structured workflows, with human escalation.
- **Beyond Presence** (AI interfaces): API-based conversational video avatars for real-time interaction with AI services.
- **SPRYT / Asa** (health AI): patient scheduling and follow-up through messaging, including missed-appointment prediction.
- **AURIMOD / VIVO** (medtech): ear-worn vagus nerve stimulation used in clinical pain treatment. KPMG finalist, Wed 11 Nov.
- **TissueLabs** (biotech): 3D bioprinting systems, biomaterials and software for engineered tissue research. KPMG finalist, Wed 11 Nov.
- **Releaf Paper** (circular materials): turns fallen city leaves into paper fibre instead of wood.
- **Greenole** (smart buildings): intelligence for building energy, HVAC and operational performance.
- **AgiBot / Galbot** (robotics): humanoid and embodied robots. AgiBot is on Centre Stage on Thu 12 Nov.

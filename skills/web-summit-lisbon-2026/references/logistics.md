## 1. Event basics

- Web Summit runs 9–12 Nov 2026 at MEO Arena and FIL, Parque das Nações. Source: [Web Summit Venue Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue-essentials/)

## 2. First arrival in 6 steps

The guide's answer to "What should I do first at my first Web Summit?":

1. **Complete your ticket.** Logging in to the Web Summit app needs a valid, completed ticket. Source: [Web Summit Login Issues](https://support.websummit.com/hc/en-us/articles/35430757733789-Login-issues); app via the [Web Summit app page](https://websummit.com/app/).
2. **Register with photo ID** at Humberto Delgado Airport T1 or Pavilhão de Portugal. Source: [Web Summit Registration Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/registration-essentials/)
3. **Collect your lanyard and wristband.** Wear both every day, at Night Summit too. Replacing a lost one costs €100. Source: [Registration Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/registration-essentials/)
4. **Pick one anchor session** using the venue map in the official app.
5. **Walk one startup area** that fits your goal.
6. **Plan a break and one quality conversation**, sequenced around your goals.

## 3. Badge pickup

- Where: Humberto Delgado Airport Terminal 1, or Pavilhão de Portugal (next to the venue). Bring photo ID and your ticket in the app. You collect a lanyard and a wristband. Source: [Web Summit Registration Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/registration-essentials/)

## 4. Getting there: airport, metro, MEO Arena

- Airport to MEO Arena: take the metro **Red Line** from Aeroporto to **Oriente** (the Parque das Nações stop), then walk to MEO Arena. Sources: [Lisbon Metro Red Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon_Metro_Red_Line), [Lisbon metro map](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/en/travel/diagrams-and-maps/)
- Uber, Bolt or taxi also work; the metro is simplest.
- Ticket: buy a **navegante occasional** card at Oriente or any metro station. Source: [Metro Lisboa](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/en/buy/viva-viagem-card/)
- Airport to the city centre by metro: stay on the Red Line to Alameda or São Sebastião.

## 5. Wifi, eSIM, luggage, bags

- Official event wifi is free throughout MEO Arena. Web Summit asks attendees **not to use personal hotspots**. Source: [Web Summit Venue Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue-essentials/)
- Add a Portugal travel eSIM as a backup (the guide's advice).
- Luggage storage at Oriente station: lockers cost about €6–9 a day. Source: [LUGGit](https://luggit.app/blog/luggage-storage-lisbon-oriente-station)
- The venue asks for **small bags only**, so store a suitcase before going in. Source: [Venue Essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue-essentials/)
